# Data handling

## Allowed

- Public material with documented origin and permitted use.
- Synthetic fixtures explicitly marked synthetic.
- Properly anonymized or redacted material approved for the repository.
- Properly licensed reference and benchmark material recorded in
  `licenses/registry.yaml`.

## Prohibited

- Live matter or client data.
- Privileged or attorney work-product content.
- Identifiable outside-counsel guidelines or engagement terms.
- Restricted legal-database content.
- Model, deployment, DMS, production, or client credentials.
- Sensitive security findings in repository artifacts or logs.

DSL-105 introduces only repository-authored placeholder text. Future fixture,
corpus, task, benchmark, and model-artifact additions must be registered and
validated under their owning issues.
