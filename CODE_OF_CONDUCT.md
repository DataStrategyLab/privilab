# Code of Conduct

PriviLAB's community includes engineers, researchers and practitioners working
in legal and regulated environments. Participants should be able to collaborate
without harassment, intimidation or pressure to disclose protected information.

## Working together

Discuss proposals and findings respectfully. Give concrete, evidence-based
feedback about the work. Make room for different expertise and accessibility
needs. Correct mistakes, credit sources and describe uncertainties honestly.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, sexual harassment, personal attacks,
doxxing, impersonation and repeated deliberate disruption are unacceptable.
Do not publish someone else's private information or submit content prohibited
by the data/security policies. Do not knowingly misrepresent capabilities,
evaluation results, source rights, professional approval or affiliations.

## Scope and reporting

This policy applies to repository issues, PRs, discussions, reviews and other
project-designated community spaces, and to official project representation.
Report conduct concerns privately to
[andrew@datastrategylab.com](mailto:andrew@datastrategylab.com). Include enough
context for review, using the minimum necessary personal information. Do not
post private reports publicly. Security concerns follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Andrew is the project contact. If a report concerns that contact or requires an
independent reviewer, request an alternative DSL reviewer through an appropriate
private DSL channel. This preview does not claim an established independent
ombuds service or a guaranteed response time.

## Maintainer response

Maintainers assess reports in context, limit disclosure to what is needed for
investigation and action, and communicate applicable next steps. Depending on
severity and recurrence, they may request a correction, issue a warning, limit
participation, temporarily suspend access or permanently exclude a participant.
Serious incidents can require immediate action. Maintainers may remove harmful
content and reject contributions that violate this policy or repository boundaries.
A participant may request reconsideration with relevant new context.

This is a project-authored policy under the repository license. It is not a copy
of the Contributor Covenant draft on the earlier public-release branch.
