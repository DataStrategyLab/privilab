# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in the PriviLAB community welcoming, safe, professional, and equitable for everyone.

PriviLAB brings together contributors from software engineering, artificial intelligence, legal technology, security, knowledge management, research, and other regulated or high-trust fields. Productive participation depends on respectful collaboration, careful evidence, and recognition of different professional responsibilities.

We are committed to an environment that respects the dignity, rights, and contributions of all participants, regardless of race, ethnicity, caste, color, age, physical characteristics, neurodiversity, disability, sex or gender, gender identity or expression, sexual orientation, language, philosophy or religion, national or social origin, socioeconomic position, education, experience level, or other status.

## Encouraged behavior

We encourage participants to:

- engage honestly, respectfully, and constructively;
- respect different viewpoints and experiences;
- give and receive actionable feedback;
- distinguish evidence, assumptions, and opinion;
- take responsibility for mistakes and correct them;
- respect privacy, confidentiality, licensing, and intellectual-property boundaries;
- focus criticism on ideas, code, architecture, evidence, or behavior rather than individuals;
- help others understand PriviLAB's security, data-handling, evaluation, and release expectations.

## Restricted behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable in PriviLAB community spaces:

- harassment, threats, intimidation, or discriminatory conduct;
- personal attacks or demeaning comments;
- sexualized behavior or unwanted sexual attention;
- deliberate disruption of project collaboration;
- publishing another person's private information without permission;
- impersonation or attempts to evade enforcement actions;
- knowingly failing to credit contributed sources where attribution is required;
- knowingly misrepresenting benchmark results, security properties, legal conclusions, affiliations, endorsements, or project capabilities;
- submitting credentials, secrets, live client information, privileged or work-product material, restricted legal content, or other data prohibited by `SECURITY.md` or `DATA_HANDLING.md`;
- conduct that threatens the safety or well-being of project participants.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in PriviLAB community spaces and when an individual is officially representing PriviLAB or Data Strategy Lab in connection with the project.

Community spaces may include GitHub issues, pull requests, discussions, code review, community events, and project communication channels designated by maintainers.

## Reporting

Do not use a public issue or pull request to disclose private information, sensitive security findings, credentials, client information, or other restricted material.

While PriviLAB remains pre-release, conduct concerns should be reported privately to the repository owner or another Data Strategy Lab maintainer.

Before the repository is made public, Data Strategy Lab will establish and publish a monitored external reporting channel for conduct concerns.

Security vulnerabilities should follow the separate process in `SECURITY.md`.

## Addressing and repairing harm

Project maintainers are responsible for interpreting and enforcing this Code of Conduct. Depending on severity, context, recurrence, and impact, maintainers may use measures including:

1. **Warning** — a private written warning and clarification of expected behavior.
2. **Temporarily limited participation** — time-limited restrictions on specified interactions or project spaces.
3. **Temporary suspension** — temporary removal from project participation, with conditions for return where appropriate.
4. **Permanent restriction** — permanent removal from project community spaces for severe or repeated violations.

Lower steps may be skipped when the seriousness of an incident warrants stronger action.

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, pull requests, discussions, or other contributions that violate this Code of Conduct or the project's security, data, licensing, or contribution requirements.

Reports will be handled as confidentially as reasonably possible while allowing maintainers to investigate and address the concern.
