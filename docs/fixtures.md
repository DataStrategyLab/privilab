# Synthetic fixtures

Fixtures provide versioned inputs for deterministic tests and local preview.
They are project-authored synthetic examples with source/hash/permitted-use
records in [licenses/registry.yaml](../licenses/registry.yaml).

- [Contexts](../fixtures/contexts/README.md): request identity/context variants.
- [Permissions](../fixtures/permissions/README.md): distinct permission-state examples.
- [Corpus](../fixtures/corpus/README.md): synthetic notice and instruction-attack records.
- [Model output guide](../fixtures/model_outputs/README.md): packaged fixed research outputs.

Do not add client/matter content, privileged material, identifiable OCG/engagement
terms, restricted legal databases or credentials. Follow the
[data policy](../DATA_HANDLING.md); document origin, rights, version and hash for
new inputs. Fixture results demonstrate control behavior, not legal conclusions.
